# Volume 6: FOLU Mapping and Sustainability Assessment

## Overview

This volume provides a comprehensive assessment of how the AFRERA platform supports the Food Systems, Land Use, and Restoration (FOLU) framework. It evaluates the platform's capabilities across healthy diets, regenerative agriculture, nature restoration, climate mitigation, water stewardship, circular economy, biodiversity, and sustainable livelihoods.

## FOLU Framework Overview

### FOLU Pillars

1. **Healthy Diets**: Promoting nutritious, diverse, and sustainable food choices
2. **Regenerative Agriculture**: Restoring soil health and ecosystem services
3. **Nature Restoration**: Protecting and restoring natural ecosystems
4. **Climate Mitigation**: Reducing greenhouse gas emissions and enhancing carbon sequestration
5. **Water Stewardship**: Ensuring sustainable water use and quality
6. **Circular Economy**: Minimizing waste and maximizing resource efficiency
7. **Biodiversity**: Protecting and enhancing agricultural biodiversity
8. **Sustainable Livelihoods**: Ensuring fair incomes and decent work for farmers

## FOLU Compliance Scoring

### Scoring Framework

**Overall FOLU Score**: Weighted average of pillar scores

**Pillar Scoring**:
- **0-30**: Poor
- **31-50**: Below Average
- **51-70**: Average
- **71-85**: Good
- **86-100**: Excellent

**Weight Distribution**:
- Healthy Diets: 10%
- Regenerative Agriculture: 15%
- Nature Restoration: 10%
- Climate Mitigation: 20%
- Water Stewardship: 10%
- Circular Economy: 10%
- Biodiversity: 10%
- Sustainable Livelihoods: 15%

### Assessment Methodology

**Data Collection**:
- Farmer practice surveys
- Soil testing data
- Carbon footprint measurements
- Water use monitoring
- Biodiversity assessments

**Scoring Process**:

```
Data Collection → Practice Assessment → 
Pillar Score Calculation → Weighted Average → 
Overall FOLU Score → Grade Assignment

```

**Grading System**:
- **A (86-100)**: FOLU Leader
- **B (71-85)**: FOLU Achiever
- **C (51-70)**: FOLU Adopter
- **D (31-50)**: FOLU Learner
- **E (0-30)**: FOLU Beginner

The AFRERA platform provides comprehensive support for the FOLU framework through its integrated modules. The platform's ability to measure, monitor, and incentivize FOLU practices positions it as a key enabler of sustainable agricultural transformation in Northeast India and beyond.
