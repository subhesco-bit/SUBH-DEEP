## Role Boundary

Lumi Earth is **one NGO partner of the project**. Lumi Earth is not the project owner, project designer, owning company, platform owner, execution partner, or sole institutional identity of the project.

The platform and project ownership remain with the Project Owner and Owning Company stated above. Other government, institutional, commercial, community, implementation, technical, funding, development, and NGO partners may be represented separately according to their actual role.
