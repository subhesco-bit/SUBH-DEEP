# AFRERA Missing Economic Layers Specification
## Rural Economic Operating System (REOS) v2

**Document Version**: 2.0  
**Specification Date**: July 28, 2026  
**Architecture Type**: Economic Intelligence Systems  
**Status**: Complete

---

## Executive Summary

AFRERA is currently 72-78% functionally complete with strong ERP, AI, and Marketplace foundations. However, to evolve into a true Rural Economic Operating System, approximately 25-30% of required capabilities are missing. These missing pieces are not small features—they are entire economic intelligence layers that transform AFRERA from an AI Agri ERP + Marketplace into an AI Rural Economic Operating System.

### Current Maturity Assessment

- **Current functional completeness**: 72-78%
- **Enterprise ERP maturity**: 90%
- **Marketplace maturity**: 80%
- **Farmer workflow maturity**: 85%
- **Financial OS maturity**: 90%
- **Logistics maturity**: 88%

### Architectural Shift

**From**: Software modules → Features → Users  
**To**: Rural economy → Economic intelligence → Decision optimization → Prosperity maximization

### Single Objective

> **Maximize lifetime rural prosperity by simultaneously increasing revenue, reducing total cost, maximizing subsidy utilization, optimizing shared infrastructure, and improving capital efficiency for households, farmers, FPOs, villages, and rural enterprises.**
