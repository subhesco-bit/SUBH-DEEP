# Farmer and family reverse-purchase business model

Farmers and consent-verified household buyers may procure household goods and agricultural inputs from any-state suppliers. Individual purchases and FPO/village buying-club aggregation share supplier terms and a delivered-cost comparison. The supplier may quote a lower bulk unit price; freight is allocated to a member's share of the pool only provisionally until the pool and carrier booking are confirmed. This is a cost-reduction and market-access model, not a guaranteed discount scheme.

`POST /api/v1/farmer-procurement/offers` lets a corporate supplier create a draft from an account-linked origin address and a validated state/service list. `POST /api/v1/farmer-procurement/offers/:id/approve` requires an admin distinct from the supplier and records the named approval. `POST /api/v1/farmer-procurement/compare` requires authentication, a farmer or verified family link, and an address owned by the caller. It checks approved supplier quotes, effective dates, served destination, MOQ and stock, selects the applicable quoted tier, and ranks feasible alternatives by provisional landed cost excluding GST, then delivery days. It saves the comparison and an append-only event. It never places an order or represents a provisional pooled quote as paid savings.

The current offer/quote tables are in migration `10011_farmer_reverse_marketplace.sql`, which still requires execution against PostgreSQL. Before supplier publication, operators must verify catalog identity, legal sale eligibility for regulated seeds/fertilizers/machinery, supplier authority, actual carrier quote and tax classification. GST, subsidies, insurance and financing are separate effective-date/eligibility calculations. A checkout may be offered only after a club's member commitment, stock hold, freight reservation, final tax and subsidy computation, payment consent, and ERP purchase/accounting references are in one confirmed transaction. Return-load capacity is an option, never a default assumption.

Existing `householdProcurementService`, `buyingClubService`, `inputSupplyManagementService` and `returnLoadBoardService` retain their distinct planning, aggregation, input and transport roles until their contracts are merged by verified caller analysis. The older buying-club preset discount schedule is **an estimate**, not an approved supplier concession; quote comparisons use only actual published supplier terms.

The shared household entry route `/household-procurement` now reads actor-scoped plans and subscriptions from `/api/v1/strategic/household`, accepts a real household/date/budget plan, and links to `/farmer-procurement` for national supply comparisons. The detail API no longer returns a fabricated success with a null plan/group. Plan creation checks that the actor is the household head; supplier comparisons separately check verified farmer/family authority. These surfaces are an integrated planning-to-sourcing journey, not an order-placement claim.

Order closure must reconcile the existing `procurement_orders` schema (`042_rural_procurement_logistics_mobility_schema.sql`) before reuse. That table has an order number, REU/club and pricing/fulfillment fields, but no authenticated buyer, farmer-family link, supplier-offer approval ID or quote-run lineage. Those identities and a final GST/freight snapshot need an explicit migration and transaction boundary. The buying-club savings rollup currently sums `procurement_orders.total_savings`; it must count only settled, evidenced discounts after the order/payment/custody lifecycle is joined. A pending quote cannot be included.
