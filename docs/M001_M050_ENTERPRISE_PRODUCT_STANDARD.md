# M001–M050 Enterprise Product Standard

This clone-only pass makes ERP/domain operations co-equal with AI. Every module has an explicit product specification covering domain focus, operational pages, state-machine workflow, sourced KPIs, visualizations, roles, integrations, middleware, ERP controls, decisions, governed AI and certification.

The domain module remains authoritative. Shared workspace services do not replace M001–M050 business services or schemas. KPI values require source references and are never fabricated. Consequential decisions require human approval and maker-checker controls.

## Certification definition
A module is certified only when its authoritative service loads, database/API/middleware contracts pass, its page renders, real workflow and authorization-negative tests pass, KPI provenance is demonstrated, accessibility/observability checks pass, AI evaluation passes where AI is used, and E2E is green.

## Scale-out
The same contract is intentionally reusable for M051 onward after M001–M050 reaches green certification. Copying the shell alone does not constitute certification.
